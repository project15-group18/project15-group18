## Project 15 · Group 18 "Mission Possible"

We are **Harshal Tittamelanahalli Venkatesh** and **Sagar Vig**, MSc Data Science students in Germany. This account hosts our research project on **when AI confidence justifies relying on a model's recommendation**.

### 🔬 Research project

**[Warranted Reliance and Uncertainty in AI](https://github.com/project15-group18/Project15-warranted-reliance)**: a reproducible study combining machine-learning experiments with a conceptual question from the philosophy of AI. The target journal is *Minds and Machines* (Springer Nature).

- **Calibration and decision costs:** when do confidence-based and cost-aware policies act differently on identical predictions?
- **Uncertainty in evidence:** how finite reliability evidence changes reliance decisions (validated Hoeffding bounds, Monte Carlo coverage checks)
- **Distribution shift:** natural state and year transport in ACS data, and recalibration without retraining
- **Subgroup burdens:** deferral and limited human review under imperfect fallback decisions
